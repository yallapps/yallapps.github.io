Principles

What every YallApps app promises.

No ads, ever

No banners, no pop-ups, no paid placements. Nothing competes with what you opened the app for.

No tracking, no account

No analytics and no sign-up. What you do in the app stays on your device.

Offline-first

Apps work without a connection. When they do go online, it is optional and explained.

Verified downloads

Every release is signed with the same key and published with SHA-256 checksums, so you can check what you install.
