Features

Understand your battery.
Then make it last.

PowerBat360 explains what is happening in plain words, then shows the actions that give you the most time back.

Time left, per app

A live estimate of your battery life based on what is running, and a simulator: tick the apps you could close and see how many minutes you would gain.

One-click optimizer

17 checks on power mode, processor boost, screen, sleep, wake timers and startup apps, with a score out of 100. Each fix explains why it matters and can be undone.

Battery health

Health, cycles and capacity, with a wear curve from Windows' own battery reports and a projection of how long your battery will stay above 80%.

Day-by-day history

How much battery you used each day, when, and which apps were responsible, compared with your usual days.

Plug and unplug rules

Switch the screen to 60 Hz and lower the brightness when you unplug, restore them when you plug in, and get warned about power-hungry apps or a low battery.

Charge limit

Detects the charge limit on Lenovo ThinkPads and explains where to set it on other brands. Staying at 100% all day is what wears a battery the most.

Questions

Good to know.

Is PowerBat360 really free?

Yes. No subscription, no in-app purchase and no advertising.

Does it work on my PC?

PowerBat360 runs on Windows 10 (version 1809 or later) and Windows 11, on laptops from any brand. It also works on desktop PCs without a battery: the optimizer, processes and power estimates are still useful there.

How accurate is the time-left estimate?

It combines the battery's real discharge rate with an estimate of each app's power use (processor, graphics, screen). The app shows a confidence level, which improves as it observes your PC on battery.

Can the optimizer break something?

It only changes standard Windows power settings, explains each one before you apply it, and keeps the previous value so every change can be undone from the same page.

Why does Windows warn me when I open it?

The preview is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen does not know it. Click More info, then Run anyway. On PCs with Smart App Control turned on, Windows may block it until signed versions are available. You can check every file with the published checksums.

Is it available in English?

Not yet. The interface is in French for now; other languages will come later.
