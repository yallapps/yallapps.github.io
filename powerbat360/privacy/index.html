Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

In short: PowerBat360 does not collect personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no network connection. Everything the app measures stays on your PC.

What the app reads on your PC

To estimate your battery life and suggest improvements, PowerBat360 reads, locally:

  • your battery's level, charge state, capacity, cycle count and Windows battery reports;
  • the running programs and their processor, graphics and memory use;
  • your Windows power, screen and sleep settings, and the apps that start with Windows;
  • basic hardware information (manufacturer, model, processor, screen) and, on Lenovo PCs, the charge limit setting.

Data stored on your PC

The app stores the following files in your user folder (%LOCALAPPDATA%\PowerBat360):

  • a usage journal, written every two minutes while the app runs (battery level, power use and the apps that used the most energy), kept for three months;
  • your settings and automation rules;
  • the previous value of every setting the optimizer changed, so you can undo it.

This information is never sent anywhere. You can erase the journal from the History page, and deleting the folder above removes everything.

Changes to your PC

The app changes Windows settings (power mode, screen, sleep, brightness, refresh rate, startup apps) only when you apply a fix or turn on an automation rule. It can close a program only when you ask it to. It runs without administrator rights.

Network

PowerBat360 makes no internet requests. Buttons that open a manufacturer's tool (for example Lenovo Vantage) launch that tool, which follows its own privacy policy.

The app files are hosted on GitHub. Like any web server, GitHub can see the technical information that comes with a download, such as your IP address, under its own privacy statement. YallApps does not receive or store this information.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party fonts or scripts, and stores nothing in your browser. It is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be asked by opening an issue on github.com/yallapps/powerbat360-releases.
