Features

Simple to learn.
Hard to put down.

A game you can play in thirty seconds, with enough depth to keep you coming back every day.

Bold calls pay more

Each call shows what it is worth: a safe guess earns a few points, an unlikely one up to fifty. Every five right calls raise your multiplier, up to ×4.

Daily Challenge

The same 20 rounds for everyone, once a day. Hit 14 to clear it, then share your result grid. A new challenge arrives at midnight.

Missions & levels

Three new missions every day, XP for every game and fifty levels to climb.

Streaks with shields

Play every day to grow your streak. Every seven days earns a shield that covers a missed day.

Badges & themes

Sixteen badges to earn and five themes to unlock, from Cyber Blue to Sunset Arcade.

Records & stats

Best score, average, accuracy, longest run and a chart of your recent days, all kept on your device.

Questions

Good to know.

Is NexNum really free?

Yes. The whole game is free: no subscription, no in-app purchase and no advertising.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. NexNum plays entirely offline. The only optional connection is the daily update check, which you can turn off in Settings › Updates.

Is the Daily Challenge the same for everyone?

Yes. The numbers of each day's challenge are generated from the date, so every player gets the same 20 rounds and can compare results.

Are there online leaderboards?

Not yet. Your records are personal and stay on your device. Sharing your Daily Challenge grid is the way to compare with friends for now.

Why is it not on Google Play?

Distributing the game directly keeps it independent and lets us ship improvements quickly. The files are signed with the same key for every release, and you can check them with the published checksums.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. NexNum is currently available for Android only.
