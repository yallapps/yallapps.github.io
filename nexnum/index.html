Features

Simple to learn.
Hard to put down.

A duel takes a couple of minutes, with enough strategy to keep you coming back every day.

A real duel

Both sides hide a number and take turns guessing. Every hint narrows the range on screen, and the first to find the other's number wins.

Three CPU levels

Easy guesses at random, Normal plays smart but slips, Hard never wastes a guess. Harder wins and fewer guesses score more.

Daily Challenge

One secret number for everyone, once a day. Find it in seven guesses or fewer, then share your arrow trail. A new number arrives at midnight.

Missions & levels

Three new missions every day, XP for every duel and fifty levels to climb. Play daily to grow a streak protected by shields.

Badges & themes

Eighteen badges to earn and five themes to unlock, from Cyber Blue to Sunset Arcade.

Records & stats

Best wins, win rate, wins by level, win streaks and a chart of your recent days, all kept on your device.

Questions

Good to know.

Is NexNum really free?

Yes. The whole game is free: no subscription, no in-app purchase and no advertising.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. NexNum plays entirely offline. The only optional connection is the daily update check, which you can turn off in Settings › Updates.

Is the Daily Challenge the same for everyone?

Yes. Each day's number is generated from the date, so every player hunts the same number and can compare how many guesses it took.

Can the CPU cheat?

No. The CPU picks its number before the first turn and the game answers every guess automatically, for both sides. Your own number is only used to answer the CPU's guesses.

How hard is the Hard level?

Hard always splits the remaining range in half, so it finds any number in seven guesses at most. To beat it you need to start first or play just as precisely.

Are there online leaderboards?

Not yet. Your records are personal and stay on your device. Sharing your Daily Challenge arrow trail is the way to compare with friends for now.

Why is it not on Google Play?

Distributing the game directly keeps it independent and lets us ship improvements quickly. The files are signed with the same key for every release, and you can check them with the published checksums.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. NexNum is currently available for Android only.
