Features

Nothing you don't.

Designed to be calm and quick to use, so that the app steps aside and the remembrance comes first.

Morning & evening adhkar

The daily remembrances as compiled in Ḥiṣn al-Muslim, plus after prayer, before sleep, upon waking and while travelling.

A reader with a counter

Read each invocation with its translation, tap to count the repetitions and move on automatically. Undo is one tap away.

Tasbih

Count your dhikr with presets or your own phrases, choose a target, and keep track of today and all time.

Gentle reminders

Local reminders at the times you choose. Permission is only requested when you turn a reminder on.

Arabic, English & French

The invocations always stay in Arabic, with translations and transliteration. The interface speaks your language.

Comfortable reading

Adjustable Arabic text size and line spacing, light and dark themes, favorites and an offline search.

Questions

Good to know.

Is Athkar 360 really free?

Yes. There is no paid version, no subscription, no in-app purchase and no advertising.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. All content is inside the app and everything works offline. The only optional connection is the daily update check, which you can turn off in Settings › Updates.

Why is it not on Google Play?

Distributing the app directly keeps it independent and lets us ship corrections quickly. The files are signed with the same key for every release, and you can check them with the published checksums.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Athkar 360 is currently available for Android only.

Where does the content come from?

The selection and wording of the invocations follow Ḥiṣn al-Muslim (Fortress of the Muslim) by Saʿīd ibn ʿAlī ibn Wahf al-Qaḥṭānī. Quranic verses are reproduced verbatim from the Tanzil Quran Text (Uthmani, version 1.1), © Tanzil Project, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0.

Are the translations reviewed?

The English and French translations were prepared for the app and are marked in the app as pending scholarly review until a qualified reviewer signs them off.
