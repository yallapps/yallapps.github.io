Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

In short: Athkar 360 does not collect personal data. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics. What you do in the app stays on your device.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the following information locally, on your device only:

  • your favorites and your daily progress;
  • your tasbih sessions and custom phrases;
  • your preferences (language, theme, reading options, reminders).

This information is never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Reminders and notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally by your device. The notification permission is only requested when you turn a reminder on, and no notification is sent from a server.

Permissions

The app does not access your location, contacts, camera or microphone. On Android it may ask to install updates, which is used only when you accept an update (see below).

Update check (Android)

Once a day, the app reads a small public file on yallapps.github.io to find out whether a new version is available. The request contains no personal data and no identifier. If you accept an update, the app downloads the installation file from github.com and checks its integrity before opening the Android installer.

These files are hosted by GitHub. Like any web server, GitHub can see the technical information that comes with a request, such as your IP address, under its own privacy statement. YallApps does not receive or store this information. You can turn the automatic check off in Settings › Updates.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party fonts or scripts. It remembers your light or dark theme choice in your browser's local storage only. It is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be asked by opening an issue on github.com/yallapps/athkar360-releases.
